Figbolt

The CRM nobody has to fill in.

Seed round · figbolt.com

The problem

Every CRM ever sold is a database your team maintains for a machine.

Salesforce and HubSpot are filing cabinets. They are excellent at storing what someone typed, and they know nothing you did not type.

What that costs

So the record is always stale, and the rep pays for it.

Updating it is not selling Log the call, write the note, set the follow-up, move the stage. Four screens for ten minutes of work.
A deal is "Proposal" because someone remembered If they were busy, the pipeline is wrong — and nobody knows which parts.
It is built for the manager The forecast report is superb. The rep filling it in gets almost nothing back.
The inversion

Figbolt makes the machine keep the record.

It reads the email, meetings, tickets and product usage you already generate, derives the customer record from them, and asks a person only for the decisions that deserve one.

What it looks like

An account page built from interactions, not from forms.

Commitments, objections, stakeholders and product requests, each extracted from a specific email or transcript line.

Trust

Every answer cites the evidence behind it.

Ask why a deal stalled and each claim links to the email or transcript it came from. When the evidence is thin, Bolt says so instead of guessing.

The loop that closes

One customer call, zero data entry.

The rep's only job is the last step.

Why it is safe to let it act

It proposes. A person decides.

A transcript is untrusted input — a customer could say anything into it. So nothing derived from one executes on its own, and the approver sees the exact effect before saying yes.

Why this is hard to copy

The moat is the plumbing, not the prompt.

Bitemporal memory Every fact knows when it was true and what superseded it, so history is answerable.
Citation validation Answers are filtered against what was actually retrieved. A model cannot cite a record it never read.
Five-gate dispatch Allow-list, permission, schema, risk, audit. An agent can never exceed its authoriser.
Learning loop Every human correction becomes a regression case the system is scored against.
